What If Everything You Drew Became Real?

At exactly 10:15 on a Monday morning, Noah drew a hamburger in the corner of his math notebook. Five seconds later, a giant, freshly cooked hamburger appeared on his desk.

He stared at it. His classmates stared at it. Even his teacher stopped talking.

"NOAH! WHY IS THERE A HAMBURGER ON YOUR DESK?"

Noah slowly looked down at his notebook, then back at the hamburger.

"I... I think I just drew lunch."

And that was the moment Noah discovered something extraordinary. Everything he drew could become real.

At first, he thought it was a joke. Maybe someone had placed the hamburger there to prank him. But then he grabbed his pencil, drew a small red ball, and watched it roll right out of the notebook.

Okay. That was impossible.

So naturally, he decided to test it.

First, he drew a brand-new smartphone. It appeared instantly. Then he drew a pair of expensive sneakers. They landed perfectly beside his desk. Next, he drew a little golden crown and placed it on his head.

"Your Majesty, the homework is due today."

Noah looked at his teacher.

"Sorry, citizens. Your king needs a little more time."

By lunchtime, he had discovered that his drawings didn't just appear. They actually worked. A car could drive, a watch could tell time, and a little dog he drew could bark and wag its tail.

He even drew a tiny dinosaur.

Unfortunately, he forgot to make it friendly.

The dinosaur immediately chased him around the classroom while his classmates screamed and climbed onto their desks.

"WHY WOULD YOU DRAW THAT?"

"I DIDN'T THINK IT WOULD BE THIS FAST!"

After a few experiments, Noah began to understand how powerful his ability was. The more detailed his drawings became, the more realistic they appeared. A simple circle became a ball, but a carefully drawn bicycle became a fully functional bicycle.

And soon, Noah started using his new power to help people.

When his best friend Ethan complained about walking to school every morning, Noah drew him a brand-new electric bicycle.

When another classmate forgot her umbrella, he quickly drew one for her.

And when Ethan lost a video game tournament, Noah drew him a golden trophy.

"Bro, you can't just draw me a trophy!"

"Why not? You deserve one."

For the first time, Noah felt like he could make the world a better place. He wasn't just a student with a pencil anymore. He could create almost anything he imagined.

But there was one problem.

He had no idea what would happen if he drew something he couldn't control.
