The group project

A few days later, your teacher announced a group assignment.

You were hoping to work with your usual friends.

Unfortunately, the teacher had other plans.

"Emma and you will work together."

You tried to look calm.

"Okay."

Emma turned toward you.

"Looks like we're partners."

"Yeah. Guess so."

She smiled.

Her shadow immediately threw both hands into the air.

You pretended not to notice.

After class, you and Emma went to the library to plan the assignment.

She opened her laptop while you started writing down ideas.

"How about we make a presentation instead of a poster?" she suggested.

"That could work."

"And maybe we can add a short video."

"That's actually a good idea."

She looked surprised.

"Wow. You agreed with me twice."

You laughed.

"Should I disagree just to make things interesting?"

"Please don't."

Her shadow was standing beside your chair.

It was giving you two thumbs-up.

You had to look away before Emma noticed you smiling.

Over the next few days, working together became surprisingly enjoyable.

Emma was organized but sometimes forgot small details.

You were good at solving problems but occasionally spent too much time worrying about minor things.

She teased you about it.

You teased her back.

And somehow, the assignment that had initially seemed like a chore became the best part of your school week.

One afternoon, you stayed late to finish the presentation.

The library was almost empty.

Emma stretched and looked at the clock.

"Wow. We've been here for two hours."

"Really? It felt like twenty minutes."

She looked at you.

"That's because you spent half the time choosing fonts."

"Fonts are important."

"Sure. Very important."

Her shadow was laughing.

Not Emma.

The shadow.

It had both hands on its stomach, bending forward as if it couldn't stop laughing.

You quickly looked down at your laptop.

This was getting ridiculous.
