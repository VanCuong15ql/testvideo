The school festival

A week later, the school announced its annual festival.

Every class had to prepare a small activity.

Your class decided to run a little game booth.

And somehow, you and Emma ended up being responsible for decorating it.

After school, you both stayed in the classroom with colorful paper, markers, tape, and a pile of cardboard.

Emma held up a sign.

"Does this look good?"

You tilted your head.

"Maybe the letters should be bigger."

She narrowed her eyes.

"You're very demanding for someone who hasn't done much."

"I've been providing creative direction."

"That's a fancy way of saying you've been sitting there."

You laughed.

She threw a small piece of paper at you.

You picked it up and threw it back.

For a few seconds, the classroom turned into a ridiculous paper battle.

Then Emma laughed so hard that she had to sit down.

You looked at her.

And for a moment, you forgot about the decorations.

Her shadow was sitting beside her, laughing too.

It looked happier than ever.

Later, while you were cutting cardboard, you accidentally nicked your finger on a rough edge.

"Ow."

Emma immediately looked over.

"What happened?"

"It's nothing."

She put down her marker and came closer.

"Let me see."

She checked your finger and brought you a small bandage from her bag.

"Here. Be careful next time."

"Thanks."

She smiled.

"You're supposed to help me finish the booth, not injure yourself."

You laughed.

Her shadow, however, had both hands on its cheeks.

It looked extremely worried.

Then it carefully reached toward your hand.

You glanced at Emma.

She was already returning to the decorations.

You couldn't help smiling.

The misunderstanding

On the day of the festival, everything was going well.

Your booth was busy.

Emma was laughing with the other students.

You were helping people play the game.

Then, during a short break, you noticed Emma standing with Daniel.

The same Daniel who had been talking to her near the school gate.

He handed her a drink.

She smiled and thanked him.

You looked away.

You knew you had no reason to feel jealous.

But somehow, it still bothered you.

Then you looked at her shadow.

It wasn't celebrating.

It wasn't smiling.

It was looking directly at you with a disappointed expression.

Then it pointed toward Emma and made a small gesture, as if asking you to come closer.

You hesitated.

Before you could decide what to do, Emma noticed you.

"Hey! Where did you go?"

"Nowhere. I was just getting some water."

She looked at you for a moment.

"Are you okay?"

"Yeah. Why?"

"You've been quiet."

You shrugged.

"Just a little tired."

Emma nodded.

Then she glanced at Daniel.

"He was just helping me carry the drinks. We ran out at the booth."

You felt slightly embarrassed.

"Oh. I see."

Emma looked at you.

"Did you think something else?"

You quickly shook your head.

"No."

She smiled.

"Okay."

But her shadow was now standing behind her, making an exaggerated facepalm.

You almost laughed.

Apparently, even her shadow thought you were being ridiculous.
