The moment she needed you

Later that afternoon, the festival became crowded.

Emma was responsible for collecting tickets at the entrance.

You were helping at the game booth.

Then you noticed her standing alone near the side of the hall.

She was looking down at her phone.

Something seemed wrong.

You walked over.

"Hey. Everything okay?"

She looked up.

"Oh. Yeah. I'm fine."

But her shadow was sitting on the floor with its head lowered.

It looked sad.

You didn't know what had happened, but you could tell she wasn't okay.

"Want to take a break?"

Emma hesitated.

Then she nodded.

You found two empty chairs near the window.

For a while, neither of you spoke.

Then she finally said:

"I think I messed something up."

"What happened?"

"I was supposed to prepare the tickets for the next activity, but I forgot to bring them."

You looked at her.

"Is that all?"

She nodded.

"I know it's not a big deal, but everyone is depending on me."

You smiled.

"Then let's fix it."

She looked surprised.

"You'll help?"

"Of course."

You both went back to the classroom and quickly prepared the missing tickets.

It took less than fifteen minutes.

When you returned, Emma looked relieved.

"Thank you."

"No problem."

Her shadow suddenly jumped up and made a heart with both hands.

You smiled.

For the first time that day, Emma looked genuinely happy again.

The confession

After the festival ended, you and Emma walked outside together.

The sky was turning orange.

Students were leaving in groups, carrying decorations and bags.

Emma was holding a small box of leftover supplies.

"Today was exhausting," she said.

"Definitely."

"But it was fun."

"Yeah. Especially the paper fight."

She laughed.

"I still can't believe you threw paper at me."

"You started it."

"I did not!"

You both laughed.

Then the conversation slowly became quiet.

You had spent so much time together over the past few weeks.

You had worked on assignments, shared an umbrella, decorated a booth, and helped each other through a stressful day.

And every time, Emma's shadow had revealed something she never said out loud.

But you still didn't know what Emma herself felt.

You finally decided to ask.

"Emma?"

"Yeah?"

"Can I tell you something?"

She turned toward you.

"Of course."

You took a breath.

"I think I've started liking you."

Emma stopped walking.

You continued before you lost your courage.

"Actually, I've liked you for a while. I just didn't know if you felt the same way."

For a moment, she said nothing.

Then she looked down.

Her shadow immediately jumped up and started celebrating.

Emma slowly looked back at you.

"I like you too."

You blinked.

"Really?"

She nodded.

"Really."

You smiled.

"So… does that mean we can go out sometime?"

Emma laughed.

"Are you asking me out?"

"Maybe."

She smiled.

"Then maybe my answer is yes."

You both laughed.

And just when you thought the day couldn't get any stranger, Emma suddenly looked down at the pavement.

Not at her own shadow.

At yours.

She stared at it for a few seconds.

Then she started laughing.

"Your shadow is really nervous right now."
