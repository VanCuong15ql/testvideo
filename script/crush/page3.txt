The rainy afternoon

The following Thursday, heavy rain started just as school ended.

Students rushed toward the entrance.

You stood under the roof, checking your phone and waiting for the rain to slow down.

Then Emma appeared beside you.

"Do you have an umbrella?"

You shook your head.

"Unfortunately, no."

She held up a small blue umbrella.

"Want to share?"

You looked at the umbrella.

Then at Emma.

"Are you sure?"

"Unless you want to swim home."

You laughed.

"Okay. Fair enough."

The umbrella was small.

Very small.

You both had to walk close together to stay underneath it.

At first, you tried to leave as much space between you as possible.

Which meant one of your shoulders was getting soaked.

Emma noticed.

"You're getting wet."

"I'm fine."

She moved the umbrella toward you.

"You're really bad at sharing an umbrella."

You laughed.

"You're the one holding it."

"Exactly. And I'm doing a terrible job."

You both laughed.

But when you looked down, her shadow was doing something strange.

It was standing right beside yours, holding an imaginary umbrella over both of you.

Then it slowly leaned its head against your shoulder.

You almost walked into a puddle.

Emma looked at you.

"Careful!"

"Yeah. Sorry."

You continued walking.

A few minutes later, Emma suddenly asked:

"Can I tell you something?"

"Sure."

"You're actually easier to talk to than I expected."

You looked at her.

"Was I difficult to talk to before?"

She laughed.

"No. I just thought you were really quiet."

You shrugged.

"I'm usually quiet around people I don't know very well."

"And now?"

You looked at her.

"Now I'm getting used to you."

Emma smiled.

Her shadow immediately started jumping.

You pretended to focus on the road.
