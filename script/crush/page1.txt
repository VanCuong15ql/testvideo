What If Your Crush’s Shadow Revealed Her Secret Feelings?

Exactly 8:12 on Monday morning, you noticed something strange.

Your CRUSH was standing on the other side of the school hallway, chatting with her friends.

You looked at her.

She smiled.

You immediately looked away.

Just another normal day.

But then you noticed something completely impossible.

Her shadow was waving at you.

You blinked.

She wasn't waving.

Both of her hands were still holding her books.

But her shadow?

It was waving like it had just spotted its favorite celebrity.

You stared at it.

The shadow pointed at you, then used both hands to make a heart.

You almost dropped your backpack.

You looked back at Emma.

She was still chatting with her friends, completely unaware of what her shadow was doing.

You quickly walked into your classroom.

There was no way you were going to spend the rest of the morning staring at someone's shadow.

At least, that was the plan.

The first strange discovery

During the first break, you were standing near the classroom window when Emma approached.

"Hey, do you understand question five?"

You turned around.

"Uh… maybe. Let me see."

She handed you her notebook.

You looked at the question, trying very hard to concentrate.

But then you noticed her shadow on the wall.

While Emma was looking at the notebook, her shadow was leaning toward you with its hands clasped together.

It looked like it was waiting for something.

You glanced at Emma.

"What's wrong?" she asked.

"Nothing. I just… need to think."

Her shadow suddenly nodded enthusiastically.

You looked away.

After explaining the question, Emma smiled.

"Thanks! I knew you'd understand it."

Her shadow immediately jumped into the air.

You nearly laughed.

"You're welcome."

Emma walked back to her seat.

Her shadow turned around and gave you one final thumbs-up before following her.

You were beginning to suspect that her shadow had a personality of its own.
