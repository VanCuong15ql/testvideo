PART 1 — THE RED STRING

Imagine waking up one morning and discovering that a red string was attached to your little finger.

You try pulling it.

It stretches.

You try cutting it.

The scissors pass straight through.

But here's the terrifying part...

The other end is connected to your crush.

And no matter how far you run, you can't escape her.

It all started on an ordinary Monday.

Alex was getting ready for school when he noticed something strange on his left hand. A thin red thread was wrapped around his little finger. It was so delicate that he almost mistook it for a loose fiber from his blanket.

He pulled it.

Nothing happened.

He tried cutting it with scissors.

Nothing.

Then he followed the thread with his eyes.

It disappeared through his bedroom door.

Alex stared at it.

"Okay... that's definitely not normal."

He followed it downstairs, out of his house, and all the way to school.

And there, standing near the entrance, was Maya.

The girl he had secretly liked for almost a year.

The red string was connected to her little finger.

Maya looked down at her hand.

Then she looked at Alex.

Then back at the string.

"Alex... why is there a red string connecting us?"

Alex had absolutely no answer.

And before either of them could say another word, the string suddenly tightened.

They were pulled toward each other.

Their foreheads collided.

And that was how the strangest relationship of Alex's life began.

PART 2 — WE CAN'T GET APART

At first, Alex thought the string was just some kind of weird prank.

But Maya wasn't laughing.

She tried walking away.

The string stretched behind her.

She took another step.

Then another.

Suddenly, it snapped tight and pulled Alex across the hallway.

He crashed into a locker.

"OW! Could you walk a little slower?"

Maya crossed her arms.

"Maybe you should stop following me!"

"I'm not following you! You're literally attached to me!"

Their argument attracted everyone's attention.

A few students started whispering.

One of them even said, "Are they dating?"

Alex immediately shook his head.

"No! Absolutely not!"

Maya looked at him.

"Wow. Thanks."

Alex realized how that sounded.

"I mean... that's not what I meant!"

But it was too late.

The school rumor had already started.

Things got worse during lunch.

Alex sat with his best friend, Sam, hoping to pretend everything was normal.

He had barely opened his lunchbox when Maya walked toward another table.

The string pulled tight.

Alex's lunchbox flew off the table.

His sandwich landed on the floor.

Sam stared at him.

"Bro... are you sure you're not dating her?"

Alex sighed.

"I wish I knew."

PART 3 — THE TEN-METER RULE

After school, Alex and Maya decided to investigate the string.

They tested everything.

Pulling it.

Twisting it.

Trying to hide it under their sleeves.

Nothing worked.

Then they discovered something even stranger.

The string had a maximum distance.

About ten meters.

Whenever they moved farther apart, it began shrinking.

And once they crossed the limit...

WHOOOSH!

They were dragged toward each other.

Maya stared at Alex.

"So we're basically stuck together?"

"Looks like it."

"Forever?"

Alex swallowed.

"Let's not use that word yet."

They tried one final experiment.

Maya stood on one side of the school courtyard.

Alex stood on the other.

They slowly walked in opposite directions.

Nine meters.

Nine and a half.

Ten.

The string began glowing.

Then it pulled them together so quickly that they both stumbled into a pile of fallen leaves.

Maya sat up and glared at him.

"Your fault."

"MY fault? You're the one who kept walking!"

And for the first time that day...

Maya laughed.

Alex couldn't help smiling.

Maybe being connected to her wasn't completely terrible.

PART 4 — THE ACCIDENTAL DATE

The next morning, Alex discovered that the string didn't just connect their hands.

It seemed to connect their schedules too.

Whenever one of them went somewhere, the other had to follow.

Maya needed to buy a notebook.

Alex had to go with her.

Maya wanted a drink.

Alex ended up standing in line.

Maya went to the library.

Alex was dragged between the bookshelves.

At one point, Maya suddenly turned toward the restroom.

Alex immediately stopped.

"Wait! Where are you going?"

Maya gave him an annoyed look.

"Do you really need to ask?"

Alex stepped backward.

"Okay! I'll wait here!"

For the first time, the string actually gave them enough room to stand apart.

But their classmates noticed them everywhere.

At the cafeteria.

In the library.

Outside the school gate.

By the end of the day, everyone was convinced they were secretly dating.

Sam approached Alex with a serious expression.

"Just tell me one thing. When were you planning to announce it?"

"Announce what?"

"Your relationship."

Alex nearly choked.

"There is no relationship!"

Sam looked at Maya, who was standing beside him.

"Then why does she look happier than you?"

Alex turned toward Maya.

She quickly looked away.

And for some reason, that made his heart beat faster.

PART 5 — THE STRING HAS FEELINGS

That night, Alex noticed another strange detail.

The string was glowing.

Not constantly.

Only when Maya was nearby.

When she laughed, it became brighter.

When she was upset, it turned dark red.

And when she looked directly at Alex...

It pulsed like a tiny heartbeat.

Alex decided to test something.

The next day, he deliberately started talking to another girl in class.

He wasn't trying to make anyone jealous.

He simply wanted to see whether the string would react.

But the moment Maya noticed them talking, the string tightened.

Alex was pulled backward.

His chair scraped across the floor.

Maya stared at him.

"What was that?"

Alex quickly stood up.

"Nothing!"

Maya narrowed her eyes.

"Are you sure?"

Alex looked down.

The string was glowing bright red.

He finally understood something.

The string wasn't just reacting to distance.

It was reacting to emotions.

But whose emotions?

His?

Maya's?

Or both?

PART 6 — THE FIRST BIG FIGHT

Later that afternoon, Maya discovered Alex searching online for ways to remove the string.

She stood behind him and read the screen.

"How to break a mysterious red string?"

Alex quickly closed the laptop.

"Maya! I can explain!"

She looked disappointed.

"Why are you trying so hard to get rid of it?"

Alex hesitated.

"Because this isn't normal. We can't stay connected forever."

Maya looked down at their hands.

"So being connected to me is that terrible?"

"No! That's not what I said."

"Then what did you mean?"

Alex struggled to find the right words.

He wanted to tell her that he was scared.

Not because he disliked being with her.

But because he didn't know whether she actually wanted to be there.

Instead, he said the worst possible thing.

"I just want things to go back to normal."

Maya went quiet.

Then she turned away.

"Fine. Me too."

The string suddenly lost its glow.

Its bright red color faded into a dark, almost black shade.

For the first time, Alex felt that something was seriously wrong.

PART 7 — THE SECRET OF THE RED STRING

That evening, Alex searched through old stories about the mysterious red thread.

He found a legend.

According to the story, an invisible red string connected two people whose lives were destined to cross.

But the legend contained something most people overlooked.

The string didn't always connect two people who were meant to fall in love.

Sometimes, it connected two people who had something important left unsaid.

Alex read the sentence again.

Something important left unsaid.

Suddenly, he remembered all the moments he had avoided telling Maya how he felt.

Every time he wanted to talk to her, he changed the subject.

Every time she smiled at him, he looked away.

Maybe the string wasn't forcing them to become a couple.

Maybe it was giving them one chance to stop avoiding the truth.

But there was another detail.

The legend said the string would disappear when both people finally understood why they had been connected.

Alex stared at the screen.

Then he received a message from Maya.

"Can we talk tomorrow?"

PART 8 — THE STRING STARTS BREAKING

The next morning, Alex met Maya at their usual spot near the school garden.

Neither of them spoke at first.

Then Maya noticed something.

A tiny crack had appeared in the red string.

Alex stared at it.

"What happened?"

Maya shook her head.

"I don't know."

Another crack appeared.

Then another.

The string was slowly breaking.

Alex suddenly felt nervous.

If it disappeared, would they go back to being strangers?

Would Maya stop talking to him?

Would everything return to the way it was before?

Maya looked at him.

"Alex, can I ask you something?"

"Yeah?"

"If this string disappeared today... would you still want to spend time with me?"

Alex froze.

For once, he couldn't hide behind a joke.

He looked directly at her.

"Yes."

Maya waited.

"Even without the string?"

Alex nodded.

"Especially without the string."

The red thread flickered.

But it didn't disappear.

Not yet.

PART 9 — THE FINAL CHOICE

Alex finally understood what he had to do.

He couldn't keep relying on the string to bring Maya closer.

He had to choose to walk toward her himself.

So he took a breath.

"Maya, there's something I've wanted to tell you for a long time."

She looked at him.

Alex continued.

"I liked you before this string appeared. I liked talking to you. I liked seeing you every morning. And honestly, I was terrified that if I told you, things would become awkward between us."

Maya didn't answer immediately.

Alex lowered his eyes.

"But I don't want to hide behind this strange thing anymore. I don't want you to stay with me because we're forced to."

The string began glowing brighter.

Maya looked at their connected hands.

Then she smiled.

"Alex... there's something I haven't told you either."

He looked up.

"I already knew you liked me."

Alex blinked.

"What?"

Maya laughed.

"You're not exactly good at hiding it."

Alex covered his face.

The string began to shine like a tiny red star.

Then, slowly...

It loosened.

PART 10 — THE REAL CONNECTION

The red string slipped away from their fingers.

For a moment, neither of them moved.

Alex looked at his hand.

It was finally free.

No pulling.

No distance limit.

No mysterious force deciding where he had to go.

He looked at Maya.

"So... I guess we're not connected anymore."

Maya smiled.

"Not by the string."

Alex wasn't sure what to say.

Then Maya reached out and took his hand.

"But that doesn't mean we can't stay together."

Alex looked at her.

"Does this mean...?"

Maya tilted her head.

"Does what mean?"

He laughed nervously.

"Never mind."

She smiled.

"You're really slow, Alex."

And just like that, they walked toward the school gate.

Side by side.

No invisible string.

No supernatural rules.

Just two people choosing to stay close.

But as Alex glanced down at his little finger, he noticed something.

A tiny red thread was still there.

It wasn't connected to Maya anymore.

It was connected to something beyond the school gate.

Alex stopped walking.

"Maya..."

She turned around.

"What?"

He pointed at his finger.

The thread stretched into the distance.

And somewhere at the other end...

Someone was holding it.
